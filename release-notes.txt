FoxTodoWidget Windows release

Install:
1. Extract the complete release folder.
2. Run install.bat.
3. The app will be copied to %LOCALAPPDATA%\FoxTodoWidget and a desktop shortcut will be created.

On first launch, choose a city or use automatic IP-based city detection.
Click the weather label later to change the city.

Right-click the fox to choose automatic carousel, a fixed built-in expression, or a local custom image. The selected mode and custom image are saved on this computer.
